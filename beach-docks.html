Boat Docks

AHPA maintains 18 slips at our private beach. It's one of the most economic ways to have a slip on the lake!

There is no ramp into the water from AHPA. Nearby boat access includes:

  • Lattins Cove Boat Launch — Free
  • Brookfield Town Beach Boat Ramp — Registration & Fee required

Dock and Slip Rules

Only Arrowhead Point Homeowners Association Members (shareholders) are eligible to rent dock space. There are 18 boat slips for the exclusive use of the non-waterfront members.

Annual slip holders and jet ski owners must complete licensing agreements and provide insurance information along with their annual slip deposit in the spring.

Waitlist Information

Current members on the list to receive a slip once an opening materializes.
