About the Board

Meet the volunteer directors who serve our community

Home › Board of Directors

The Board of Directors are all resident volunteers committed to preserving the unique Arrowhead charm and fostering a genuine spirit of caring, cooperation and community. The Board tries to maintain a balance of both men and women as well as lakefront and non-lakefront property owners. As the terms of the various board positions rotate regularly, we are always looking for residents to express interest in joining the team. Business and/or community service experience are helpful. Please reach out to the board at the email below to let us know if you'd like to be considered for a future opening.

David Pinto

President
president@aphact.com
Oversees the APHA Board of Directors, Committee Chairs, and community operations. Leads quarterly Board meetings and the annual membership meeting in late June. Oversees real estate transactions, provides resale documents, and serves as primary communications conduit to the community.

Chad Hossan

Vice President — Dock Master
vp1@aphact.com
Communicates with current and prospective slip holders, coordinates docks installation in spring and removal in fall, and ensures proper maintenance of lockers and the boat and swim docks including annual UV coating for boat dock.

Bob Forsberg

Vice President — General
vp2@aphact.com
Supports the rest of the Board as needed and leads and manages special projects. Oversees the social and new-resident welcome committees.

Scott Hartman

Treasurer
treasurer@aphact.com
Coordinates with outside bookkeeper to ensure vendor invoices are accurate and paid promptly. Prepares the annual budget, reports on financial performance, and reviews expenses, contracts and proposals from vendors and contractors throughout the year.

Lisa Cerbone-Montalto

Secretary
secretary@aphact.com
Maintains the membership database and contact information, and provides agenda, notes, and minutes for all official Board meetings. Coordinates with the President to co-sign and distribute stock certificates for new residents.
