Buying & Selling

Important HOA information for homeowners and real estate agents

Home › Buying & Selling

Arrowhead Point is a wonderful place to live, and we typically see about four homes sell each year. As a private community, there are some unique aspects of buying and selling that homeowners, buyers and real estate agents should be aware of.

Resale Document

Most importantly, the HOA Board will need to provide a "resale document" which will be necessary for a smooth closing. That document will include information about the property's membership in the association.

For Sellers

We ask that sellers and/or their real estate agents reach out to board@aphact.com with the following information at least three weeks before closing:

  • Seller's name and contact information
  • Property address
  • Expected closing date
  • Real estate agent contact information (if applicable)

For Buyers

If buyers or buyers' real estate agents have any questions, please email board@aphact.com and we will gladly assist.

Tennis Association

Note: Some homes in Arrowhead Point are also members of the private Arrowhead Point Tennis Association LLC. If the home being purchased includes a tennis membership, that is a separate transaction handled between buyer and seller. Not every home in Arrowhead Point belongs to the tennis association.
