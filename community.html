Beachfront & Docks

AHPA maintains 18 boat slips at our private beach — one of the most affordable ways to have a slip on Candlewood Lake. The community beach features a protected swimming area, swim dock, picnic tables, and open beach space.

Boat access: There is no ramp into the water from AHPA. Nearby options include:

  • Lattins Cove Boat Launch (Free)
  • Brookfield Town Beach Boat Ramp (Registration & Fee required)

Dock waitlist: The waitlist is maintained by the Dock Manager. Cost to join the waitlist is $500.00 (nonrefundable). Contact the Dock Manager at vp1@aphact.com for more information.

Tennis Association

Back in 2004, a group of 13 Arrowhead Point tennis enthusiast residents purchased an empty lot at 3 Myron Avenue and formed the Arrowhead Point Tennis Association LLC as a private sub-association. In 2024, the Tennis Association added pickleball nets.

Member/owners can sell their share in the tennis LLC to anyone living in Arrowhead Point at a price mutually agreeable to both buyer and seller.

Buying & Selling

Arrowhead Point typically sees about four homes sell each year. The HOA Board will need to provide a "resale document" which will be necessary for a smooth closing. Sellers and/or their real estate agents should reach out to board@aphact.com at least three weeks before closing.

Community Testimonials

Wonder what it's like to live on Arrowhead Point? Read what our residents say.

"Growing up in Arrowhead Point was like living in a slice of paradise. I recall vividly the expansive fields, where now stand rows of homes, as the backdrop for our daily adventures. Every afternoon after school, about 20 of us neighborhood kids would gather, ready to explore and play until the evening air signaled it was time to head home. There were no cell phones back then; instead, each parent had a distinct bell they would ring when it was time to return."

"The community dock was our gathering spot. We must have caught hundreds of sunfish over the years, using a little blue plastic boat as our makeshift aquarium. At the end of the day, we would release the fish, with the 'survivors' swimming free. The adults too found joy in the water, racing their sunfish sailboats across the lake. The annual beach party was another highlight, where we played football in the shallows with a greased watermelon, laughter filling the air."

"Even now, it warms my heart to see children playing on the beach, walking to the bus stop, or riding their bikes. Arrowhead Point continues to be a place where memories are made, generation after generation."

— Robin R.

"It's hard to express how welcoming and wonderful this community is. My wife and I moved here a few years ago and we've gotten to know so many great and caring people. We feel safe and secure, and we enjoy our daily walk with our dogs as we pass so many smiling faces. It has been so nice to see the many recent investments that neighbors are making in their homes. Everything is so charming and well maintained. I'm always proud to tell friends that I live on Arrowhead Point!"

— Peter M.

"We have lived in Arrowhead for nearly 8 years. While our house is not on the water, we have access to the community beach and use it all summer long. It's so nice to just pop over with the kids and have a picnic while they swim and play ball. The recent upgrades to the lockers and kayak racks (among other things) are amazing. My mom reminds me whenever she visits that we have found a VERY special community. Every time I drive into the neighborhood, I feel my blood pressure go down and I smile as if I am now on vacation. The lake is so magically calming and tranquil."

— Lauren B.

Lake Information & Organizations

The Candlewood Homeowners Alliance is committed to working cooperatively with the organizations below that influence lake policy and our quality of life. We consider them to be our partners in lake stewardship and conservatorship — not our adversaries. The Candlewood Lake Authority in particular plays a vital role in protecting the lake and ensuring public safety.

We encourage CHA members to support them financially. The link to the WestConn Lake Symposium Series provides recordings of educational sessions hosted by the Biology department — a great resource for Candlewood homeowners to understand and monitor the state of the lake's health and local ecological influences.
